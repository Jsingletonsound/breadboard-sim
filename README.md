# Breadboard Sim

An interactive breadboard simulator for people learning electronics from zero. Drag parts onto a model of the class kit's 830-point board, clip on a 9 V battery, and a real circuit solver decides what lights, how bright, and what burns out.

Open it: https://jsingletonsound.github.io/breadboard-sim/

Built for MTEC 150 (Acoustics and Audio Electronics) at MiraCosta College by James Singleton. The guided labs follow the class kit and reference page numbers in Forrest M. Mims III, *Getting Started in Electronics*. The numbers come from the simulation; they are not bench measurements. The app is not affiliated with or endorsed by the book's author or publisher.

It runs entirely in your browser: no account, no data collected. You can also save this page and open it offline.
